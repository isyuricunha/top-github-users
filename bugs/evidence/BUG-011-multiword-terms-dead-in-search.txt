Evidence for BUG-011 (sweep F1): whitespace-containing location terms are dead on GitHub search

Code: pipeline/src/collector.ts:8-10 (normalizeLocationTerm replaces whitespace with underscore) and :12-15 (buildSearchQuery emits location:<term> unquoted).

Anonymous REST probes (api.github.com/search/users, 2026-11-02 ~14:38 UTC; gh-ella token expired, counts are public):
location:united_states -> 1            | location:"united states" -> 295954
location:hong_kong -> 6                | location:"hong kong" -> 27898
location:san-francisco -> 88541        | location:"san francisco" -> 88541   (control: hyphen == quoting, works)
location:nassau -> 166                 | location:manama -> 215
location:marino -location:"san marino" -> 12

Config census (probe over config.json, 138 locations): 15 countries with whitespace terms are query-dead
(bosnia and herzegovina, burkina faso, hong kong, dominican republic, el salvador, new zealand, san marino,
saudi arabia, sierra leone, south africa, south korea, sri lanka, united arab emirates, united kingdom,
united states) plus 1 city term with whitespace: brazil/'rio grande do norte'. The 91 hyphenated city
terms in config are unaffected (control above). Affected countries still collect 500 users through their
city terms (bosnia_and_herzegovina.json and burkina_faso.json, both collected 2026-11-02, verified live).
